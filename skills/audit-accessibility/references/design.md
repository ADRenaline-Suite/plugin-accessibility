[🏠 Accessibility](../../../README.md)

A screen is approved with no name, no focus order, and no contrast note.

## Steps

1. The source is an accepted handoff or a mock the person pointed at. Without it, write nothing.
2. For each interactive element, record the accessible name, the focus order, and whether contrast and target size are specified.
3. A missing specification is a gap. Do not invent a ratio.

## Rules

1. Do not draw a replacement screen.
